General information

The Iguazu Falls are big waterfalls of Iguazu River on the border of Misiones and the Brazilian state of Paraná. they make up one of the largest waterfall system in the world. The name "Iguazu" comes from the Guarani or Tupi words meaning "water."

The location

The waterfalls can be reached from two main towns: Puerto Iguazú, in Argentina and in Foz do Iguaçu, in Brasil, as well as from Ciudad del Este, Paraguay, on the other side of the Paraná River from Foz do Iguaçu, each of those three cities having commercial airports.
